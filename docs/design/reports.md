# Reports

Reports (battle, field battle, camp, trade) belong to the players involved, not to a settlement. A player's inbox lists them across all their settlements in a world. Deleting a settlement does not delete its reports.

A report is unread for a player until that player opens it. "Mark read" marks all of that player's unread reports read. Each player of a report (attacker and defender, both trade partners) has their own read state.
