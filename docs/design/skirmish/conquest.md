# Conquest from the sea

Part of [Skirmish](README.md). The landing base is the [beach camp](beach-camp.md); what happens to a realm once it is attacked is
in [Taking over a realm](realm-takeover.md).

## Ships
- Fleets and land troops never fight each other. An attack is fought as separate fights, ships against ships, land troops
  against land troops, ships first.
- Ships do no damage to buildings on land. The Fire Ship (Brandschiff) damages buildings in the water: docks and fishers. It does
  no damage to units, cannot carry anything and eats a lot of food (high upkeep). It is single use: when it reaches the target and
  the ship fight there is won, it burns up and damages the building. In the ship fight it counts as a ship with its own hull and
  can be sunk, but adds no attack.
- Attack on a dock: ship fight only, and no loot. Attack on a beach camp: ship fight first, then the land fight. The land troops
  fight from on board and do not land. If the attacker loses the ship fight, there is no land fight. The camp's buildings are
  damaged by land siege in the land fight, never by ships.
- If the target has no fleet, the ship fight is skipped and counts as won.
- Wildlife camps follow the same split. Water camps (the whale road) are fought by fleets only. Sand camps (seal and walrus
  haul-outs) are fought by land troops only, from on board; the fleet does no fighting there. Loot is taken if the camp is cleared.
- Loot, including the food that refills the army's provisions ([troop-movement-range.md](../troop-movement-range.md) §1b), is only
  taken if the attacker wins every fight of the attack.
- Land troops ride on ships, the same way settlers do. Each ship has room for a number of slots and each unit takes some:
  cheap units 1, stronger ones up to 4, siege engines 10 (numbers: [economy.md](../economy.md), "Fleets").
- A fleet with troops on board moves at the speed of its ships.
- When a ship sinks on the way or anywhere else, the troops on it drown. Troops that are ashore do not. If the ships left
  afterwards cannot carry all the troops ashore home, the ones left over are lost.

## Support from allies
- A supporting army eats its own travel food on the way. While it stands at an ally's settlement, the ally feeds it.
- The host can send a supporting army home at any time.
