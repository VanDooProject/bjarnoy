# Taking over a realm

Part of [Skirmish](README.md).

- You attack a realm by destroying its buildings. Destroying its Longhouse ends the realm. The Longhouse can stand on sand right
  next to the shore.
- When its Longhouse is destroyed, a settlement has no claim at all.
- Its towers then lose their claim too, unless they stand within the claim of another Longhouse of yours.
- It also loses its sight: nothing around its centre is visible any more.
- Destroying the realm does not give you the land. To hold it, you place your own Longhouse and towers.
- Buildings that stand in no realm degrade over time.
