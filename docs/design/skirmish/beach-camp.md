# Beach camp

Part of [Skirmish](README.md). The base for landing from the sea (see [Conquest from the sea](conquest.md)).

- Camp builders set up the camp. They are trained like settlers (e.g. at the Cart Workshop) and ride on the fleet.
- Building a camp costs resources and takes build time, clearly less than a Longhouse.
- A camp can only go on a shore with no enemy buildings on it, so the enemy's shore buildings have to be destroyed first.
- While the camp is built, the troops wait on the camp's hex and the ships wait one hex out on the water.
- A camp may stand inside an enemy realm. It claims only its own hex, there too.
- A camp is a settlement of its own: a one-hex settlement with a stock, garrisons and no production. It follows the rules of a
  settlement with a Longhouse, with two differences: its claim is always its own hex only, and it does not count as a Longhouse
  for building requirements (it is a different building).
- A camp links with your other settlements like any settlement: when its hex touches one of your realms, it joins that realm
  and shares its stock, and it is shown under that realm's name.
- Destroying the camp ends it, the same way destroying a Longhouse ends a realm.
- Land troops fight from the camp and eat its food. The food comes in with the fleet, and the camp can be supplied again later.
- If the camp is destroyed, the troops that landed from it starve.
- A fleet that reaches the camp is garrisoned there like any other troops. It defends the camp and can be sent out again from it.
- The camp can be attacked from land and from sea. If no troops defend it, it can be plundered. If no troops are in it, it can be
  destroyed without catapults or rams.

## Numbers
Starting values, to be tuned.
- Camp builders are trained at the Cart Workshop: 80 wood, 40 brick, 40 food, 20 iron, 12 min each. Their price does not grow
  with the number of settlements.
- A camp needs 2 camp builders, costs 200 wood and 100 brick (paid when the fleet sets out) and takes 10 min to build.
- A camp holds up to 2,000 of each resource: wood, stone, food and iron, each in its own pile. When an army reaches the camp, its
  loot and leftover provisions are deposited into the camp's piles. What does not fit under a pile's limit is wasted. An army
  setting out from the camp refills its provisions to full from the camp's food pile, unless the player sets another amount in the
  dispatch panel.
- When the camp's food runs out or the camp is destroyed, the troops that landed from it starve the same way troops do in a
  settlement without food.
- A camp has no levels and cannot be upgraded.
- Plundering a camp takes whatever its piles hold, within the plundering army's loot capacity.
