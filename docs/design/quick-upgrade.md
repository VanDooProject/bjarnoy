# Quick upgrade

Premium only. Upgrades a building's next level without going through the ring menu.

## Desktop: Shift+click
- While Shift is held, a click on one of your own buildings orders its next level. A click without Shift opens the ring as before.
- While Shift is held, the building markers show the upgrade state (gold ⇈: can be ordered now, clock: has to wait) and the
  hovered building's marker box shows the next level's cost and build time.

## Quick upgrade mode
- On: hold the Upgrade bubble in a building's ring menu. A gold ring fills around it; when it closes, that building's upgrade
  is ordered and the mode is on. Letting go earlier is a normal Upgrade.
- While on: a tap on one of your own buildings orders its next level; each tap one more level. A tap on anything else opens
  the ring. Markers show the upgrade state as with Shift.
- Off: a long-press anywhere on the map (buildings included), the ✕ on the mode chip, Esc, leaving the settlement view,
  switching settlement, opening a modal or using any other part of the HUD (the build queue excepted).

## Blocked upgrades
- A click or tap on a building that cannot be upgraded (resources, slots, locks, max level, busy hex) sends nothing and
  shows the same reason the Upgrade bubble gives.
- The building's marker plays the refusal animation of a refused order's dial (shake, pop, red flash, two shockwaves), without
  the cross and without fading out: the marker stays as it was.
