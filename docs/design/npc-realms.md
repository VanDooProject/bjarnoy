# AI realms (NPC jarls)

Realms owned by the game instead of a player, run by a coded (not LLM) AI. Design track: issue #125.

## First version

- Two kinds of AI realm: **peaceful** and **hostile**.
- A peaceful realm never attacks.
- A hostile realm never attacks first. It strikes back only at a player who attacked it.
- AI realms act only on their own island. They have no ships.

## Sight

- An AI realm sees only what its own Longhouse and towers see, like a player.
- It learns about the rest of its island by sending troops out to scout.

## When the Longhouse falls

- Destroying an AI realm's Longhouse ends the realm, like a player's. It does not come back, and the land is free to settle.

## Standing

- AI realms are not on the leaderboards, cannot join guilds and cannot win the age.
