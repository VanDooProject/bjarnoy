# Garrison

- Land troops garrison in three places: longhouse, tower, beach camp. Fleets garrison in two: dock, beach camp.
- Land troops cannot garrison in a dock; they go out onto the next land tile.
- A fleet that arrives home folds into the garrison. Leftover provisions go into the stock there: the settlement stock at a dock,
  the camp's piles at a beach camp ([troop-movement-range.md](troop-movement-range.md) §1a).
