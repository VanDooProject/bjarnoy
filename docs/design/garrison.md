# Garrison

- Land troops garrison in three places: longhouse, tower, beach camp. Fleets garrison in dock and beach camp, and in a
  longhouse that stands on sand next to the sea.
- Land troops cannot garrison in a dock. They go out onto the land tile with the best route to the nearest garrison spot
  (tower or longhouse) and march there on their own.
- An army that arrives home folds into the garrison. Leftover provisions go into the stock there: the settlement stock at a
  longhouse, tower or dock, the camp's piles at a beach camp ([troop-movement-range.md](troop-movement-range.md) §1a).
- A fleet's home is the garrison spot it last folded into. A fleet that reaches another settlement of its own with a dock, or
  a beach camp of its own, folds in there, and that becomes its new home.
- The round-trip food check of an army setting out from a beach camp is measured from the camp.
- Garrisons eat from the stock of their garrison spot, ships included.
- An army whose home is destroyed (for example a beach camp while its troops are out) gets the nearest garrison spot of its
  owner as its new home.
