# Combat: which values a fight uses

## 1. Location sets the values

- In every fight between players, the fight hex decides which stat each side uses. A side whose territory covers the hex
  defends with its Defense plus the tower bonus. A side whose territory does not cover it fights with its Attack.
- When neither side or both sides claim the hex, both fight with their Attack.
- "Its territory" is the territory of the side's own realm and of its guildmates' realms.
- This holds for attacks, raids, ship fights and field battles alike.

## 2. The order sets the losses on the target hex

- An army's order (Attack, Raid) matters only in the fight on its target hex. There an Attack is a full battle (the loser
  loses everything), and a Raid is capped (neither side loses more than half).
- Every fight on the way, such as two armies meeting, is capped like a raid.

## 3. Who fights

- Two armies of the same owner that stand on the same hex join into one army. Armies of the same owner that are moving are
  not joined.
- Armies of the same guild or of guilds with an active peace treaty never fight each other.
